## Exercise 1: License Detective

| Project | License | Commercial Use | Attribution | Modify and Redistribute | Special Requirements |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [TensorFlow](https://github.com/tensorflow/tensorflow) | Apache 2.0 | Yes | Yes | Yes | Keep license and copyright notices. State changes. |
| [Scikit-learn](https://github.com/scikit-learn/scikit-learn) | BSD 3-Clause | Yes | Yes | Yes | Keep copyright notices. No endorsement using creators' names. |
| [Keras-contrib](https://github.com/keras-team/keras-contrib) | MIT | Yes | Yes | Yes | Keep original copyright and license notice. |
| [PyTorch](https://github.com/pytorch/pytorch) | BSD-style | Yes | Yes | Yes | Keep required license and copyright notices. |
| [Ultralytics YOLO](https://github.com/ultralytics/ultralytics) | AGPL 3.0 | Yes, with conditions | Yes | Yes, with conditions | Follow AGPL source-sharing rules. |

## Short Comparison

MIT and BSD licenses are flexible. They allow commercial use and modifications.

Apache 2.0 also provides patent protection.

AGPL 3.0 has stronger rules. Users must share covered source code in certain situations, including some network uses.

## Exercise 2: Build Your Own License

### EmotionAI - Text Emotion Recognition License


### 1. Who Can Use It?

Anyone can use our AI model, including students,
researchers, developers, and companies.

### 2. What Can Users Do?

Users can copy, modify, improve, and share the model.
Commercial use is also allowed.

### 3. What Must Users Do?

Users must give credit to the original creators.
They must include this license when sharing the model.
They must explain any changes they make.
Sharing improvements is optional.

### 4. What Is Not Allowed?

Users cannot claim they created the original model.
They cannot remove copyright information.
They cannot use the creators' names for advertising
without permission.

### 5. What Happens If Someone Breaks the Rules?

Users must fix any license violation within 30 days
after receiving notice.
If they do not fix it, their permission ends.

### Explanation of Our Choices

We chose a flexible license because we want people
to use and improve our AI model.

Commercial use is allowed because companies may
create useful products with the model.

Attribution is required to protect the original
creators.

We do not require users to share improvements
because we want the model to be easy to use.

This is a custom educational license, not a
standard open-source license.
